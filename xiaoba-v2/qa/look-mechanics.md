# 小八 look mechanics

小八 is a compact, front-facing, rounded cat-like mascot. Direction should read primarily through coordinated pupils, eyelids, nose/mouth placement, and a subtle head turn. The blue ear-cap marking, white rounded body, pink cheeks, black outline, tiny blue tail, overall scale, and baseline must remain unchanged.

- `000 up`: pupils and face aim visibly upward; head tips back slightly without rotating the whole body.
- `090 screen-right`: pupils, nose/mouth, and face surface shift unmistakably toward the viewer's right; the head follows subtly.
- `180 down`: pupils and face aim visibly downward; chin dips slightly.
- `270 screen-left`: pupils, nose/mouth, and face surface shift unmistakably toward the viewer's left; the head follows subtly.
- Intermediate poses interpolate clockwise in even 22.5-degree steps.
- Keep feet planted and the body centered. The tiny tail remains attached and follows the body naturally.
- No whole-sprite rotation, new props, detached effects, shadows, labels, guide marks, or identity changes.
